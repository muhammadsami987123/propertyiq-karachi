About PropertyIQ

PropertyIQ is a visual intelligence layer for Karachi's real-estate market — a way to see how price, rent, and market momentum vary block by block across the city, and to understand how confident to be in each figure.

It is not a listings site. You will not find individual properties for sale or rent here, and PropertyIQ has no commercial relationship with any listing, agent, or developer.

It is not a chatbot. There is no assistant to converse with — the product is the map, the numbers, and the transparency around where those numbers come from.

What it is: a single place to explore, compare, and reason about Karachi localities using the best available market data, an investment calculator grounded in real inputs, and a methodology that says plainly what we know, what we estimate, and what we don't know yet.
